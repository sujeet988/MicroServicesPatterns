ChreoGraphy :
Choreography means services communicate by events, not by calling each other directly.
Choreography means services talk to each other by sending and listening to events.
There’s no central boss — each service just does its part when something happens.

Example : using service bus we can  implement this.

*How It Works:
Service A finishes a task and publishes an event – e.g., OrderPlaced.
Service B is listening for OrderPlaced, and when it sees it, it reacts – e.g., SendInvoice.
Service C is also listening and may do something else – e.g., ReserveInventory
