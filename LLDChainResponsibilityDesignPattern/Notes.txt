
** ChainResponsibilityDesignPattern:
Chain of Responsibility lets you pass a request along a chain of handlers.
Each handler decides whether to process the request or pass it to the next.

Example :
Imagine a support system:

Level 1 Support → checks if it can handle
If not, passes to Level 2 Support
Then to Manager, and so on...

Each one either handles or forwards the request.
